# WarrantLab research programme

This folder holds the research programme that follows the frozen hackathon proof of concept (Git tag `hackathon-poc-v0.1`). The hackathon planning documents in [`../devpost/`](../devpost/) are historical records and are not updated for the research programme.

**Project status.** WarrantLab is the project owner's independent personal research project:
- It is not part of a university research programme, has no supervisors, and is not institutionally governed or funded.
- The project owner is responsible for its scientific direction and makes all final research decisions.
- External IS scholars are welcome as reviewers, advisers or future collaborators.

## Conventions

- Source provenance keeps four fields separate: metadata verification, full-text access, agent-assisted synthesis, and researcher verification (see `SOURCES.md`). Agent reading is never presented as researcher verification.
- Statements are labelled **[Verified]**, **[Hypothesis]**, **[Proposed principle]**, **[Open question]** or **[Plan]**.
- Novelty, accuracy and usefulness are not claimed until they have been evaluated against strong baselines.
- No unpublished manuscript is processed with the current Gemini free-tier configuration.
- Academic English, Australian spelling.
